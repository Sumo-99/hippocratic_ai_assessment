# turn 0: Alice and her cat Bob

The Whispering Book

Alice lived near a big old library. At night, the library grew quiet and soft. Bob, Alice's cat, liked to sit on the windowsill and watch it.

One night, Alice and Bob tiptoed through the library doors. The lamps were low and golden. The shelves stretched up, up, up, like tall trees.

Then they heard it. A soft whisper came from a book on the bottom shelf. "Follow me," the book said. "Follow me, softly."

Alice blinked. "Did that book just talk?"

Bob twitched his tail. Then he padded after the whisper, and Alice followed him.

The book slid open on its own. Its pages fluttered like little wings. They flew down a dim hallway, and Alice and Bob hurried to keep up.

At the end of the hallway was a small wooden door. It had a brass knob shaped like a crescent moon. The book hovered beside it and sighed, "Open me, and you will find the forgotten tales."

Alice turned the knob, slow and careful. The door creaked, just a little, and swung open.

Inside was a round room with cushions as soft as clouds. Shelves curved all the way around, filled with books that were faded and well loved. Some had lost their covers. Some had ribbons tied around them.

"These are the bedtime stories nobody has read in years," whispered the book. "Every night, they wait for a sleepy listener."

Bob hopped up onto a cushion and curled his paws under his chest. He made a happy, rumbly purr.

Alice picked up a small blue book. Its title shimmered: The Sleepy Lantern. She opened it, and a gentle glow spilled out, like a candle in a window.

"Would you read it to me?" Alice asked the book.

The pages turned by themselves. A soft voice began to read.

"Once, there was a lantern that lived on a hill. Each night, it lit the way home for birds, and bees, and tired little foxes..."

Alice yawned. Bob's purr grew slower and deeper.

The lantern in the story dimmed its light, one little bit at a time. The stars came out, one by one. The wind hummed a lullaby through the tall grass.

Alice rested her head on a cushion. The glow of the book warmed her cheeks. Her eyelids felt heavy as feathers.

"Goodnight, lantern," Alice murmured. "Goodnight, Bob."

Bob tucked his nose under his tail. The whispering book closed its covers with a soft, final sigh.

Outside, the library kept its quiet watch. The shelves stood still, and the moon peeked in through the window.

And Alice and Bob fell fast asleep, safe in the hidden room of forgotten tales.
