# turn 0: A story about a girl named Lily and her friend, a clumsy dragon

Ember and the Sleepy Sky

Lily lived in a small house at the edge of a green hill. One morning, a dragon came to visit her.

The dragon was named Ember. Ember was big and warm and shaped like a friendly teapot. Ember had a long tail, and that tail was the problem.

"Hello, Lily!" said Ember. Swish went the tail. Crash went the flowerpot.

"Oh no," said Ember. "Sorry, sorry, sorry!"

Lily giggled. "It's okay, Ember. Pots can be fixed. Let's pick up the pieces together."

So they did. Lily found the big pieces. Ember found the small pieces with one careful claw. Together they put the flowers back into the soil.

Next, they went for a walk up the hill. The grass was tall and tickled their toes. Birds sang high in the trees.

Ember stepped on a loose rock. Wobble, wobble, slide! Ember sat down very hard. Bump!

"Are you hurt?" asked Lily.

"No," said Ember. "Only my pride is bruised. My pride is a very small thing."

Lily laughed so much she had to sit down too. They sat side by side, looking at the sky.

A little breeze came by. It smelled of warm bread from the village below. Ember sniffed the air. "Mmm. That smells like a good dinner."

"After our walk, we will go home and eat soup," said Lily. "Mama makes the best carrot soup."

"Dragons like soup," said Ember. "Especially when it is not on the floor."

Lily giggled again. Ember grinned a wide, toothy grin.

They walked slowly back down the hill. The sun was getting low. The sky turned pink, then gold, then soft blue.

At the house, Lily's mama had made the soup. It was warm and orange and smelled like home. Ember had a bowl, and Lily had a bowl. Ember ate very carefully, with one claw held out like a tiny tray.

"Careful," said Lily. "Your tail is near the cupboard."

Ember tucked the tail around its feet. "There. Now I am a neat dragon."

After soup, Lily yawned. Ember yawned too, a big yawn with a little puff of smoke at the end.

"Smoke rings!" said Lily sleepily. "Good night, Ember."

"Good night, Lily," said Ember softly. "Thank you for helping with the pots and the rocks and the soup."

Lily climbed into bed. The room was quiet. The moon came up over the hill.

Ember curled up in the garden, under the apple tree, with its tail wrapped snugly around its feet. The crickets hummed their slow songs. The flowers stood tall in their fixed pot.

And both friends fell fast asleep, warm and safe, until morning.

The end.

# turn 1: make the dragon funnier

Ember and the Sleepy Sky

Lily lived in a small house at the edge of a green hill. One morning, a dragon came to visit her.

The dragon was named Ember. Ember was big and warm and shaped like a friendly teapot. Ember had a long tail, and that tail was the problem. It had a mind of its own. It liked to wiggle when Ember was not looking.

"Hello, Lily!" said Ember. Swish went the tail. Crash went the flowerpot. Then the tail gave a tiny, proud wiggle, as if it had done something clever.

"Oh no," said Ember. "Sorry, sorry, sorry! My tail did it on purpose. I am sure of it. It is a very naughty tail."

Lily giggled. "It's okay, Ember. Pots can be fixed. Let's pick up the pieces together."

So they did. Lily found the big pieces. Ember found the small pieces with one careful claw. Ember picked up a tiny piece, then sneezed, and the sneeze went PFFT and blew the piece right back onto the floor.

"Well," said Ember, "that piece is not going anywhere it did not want to go."

Lily laughed so hard she hiccupped. Together they put the flowers back into the soil.

Next, they went for a walk up the hill. The grass was tall and tickled their toes. Birds sang high in the trees. One bird landed on Ember's head and sang right into one of its ears.

"Hello, bird," said Ember, very politely. "Your song is loud. My ear is now a little bit fizzy."

Ember stepped on a loose rock. Wobble, wobble, slide! Ember sat down very hard. Bump! A little puff of smoke came out, followed by a small, surprised toot.

"Are you hurt?" asked Lily.

"No," said Ember. "Only my pride is bruised. My pride is a very small thing. It fits in a teacup, and it is wobbling right now."

Lily laughed so much she had to sit down too. They sat side by side, looking at the sky.

A little breeze came by. It smelled of warm bread from the village below. Ember sniffed the air, sniffed again, and then sniffed Lily's hair by mistake. "Mmm. That smells like a good dinner. Also, your hair smells like a good dinner."

"After our walk, we will go home and eat soup," said Lily. "Mama makes the best carrot soup."

"Dragons like soup," said Ember. "Especially when it is not on the floor, and not on my tail, and not on my face."

Lily giggled again. Ember grinned a wide, toothy grin, with a crumb of bread stuck between two teeth.

They walked slowly back down the hill. The sun was getting low. The sky turned pink, then gold, then soft blue.

At the house, Lily's mama had made the soup. It was warm and orange and smelled like home. Ember had a bowl, and Lily had a bowl. Ember ate very carefully, with one claw held out like a tiny tray. Then the spoon flew out of the claw, spun twice in the air, and landed in Lily's mama's hat.

"Oh!" said Ember. "That was a very fancy spoon trick. I did not practice it. It just happened."

"Careful," said Lily. "Your tail is near the cupboard."

Ember tucked the tail around its feet. "There. Now I am a neat dragon. A very neat dragon. Neat as a folded napkin."

After soup, Lily yawned. Ember yawned too, a big yawn with a little puff of smoke at the end, and a tiny squeak in the middle.

"Smoke rings!" said Lily sleepily. "Good night, Ember."

"Good night, Lily," said Ember softly. "Thank you for helping with the pots and the rocks and the soup. And for not laughing at my squeak. Much."

Lily climbed into bed. The room was quiet. The moon came up over the hill.

Ember curled up in the garden, under the apple tree, with its tail wrapped snugly around its feet. The crickets hummed their slow songs. The flowers stood tall in their fixed pot.

And both friends fell fast asleep, warm and safe, until morning.

The end.
