You review a bedtime story written for children aged 5 to 10, to be read aloud by a parent. Judge the story exactly as it is. Be strict but fair: pass a dimension when it is clearly met, fail it when it is not.

## How to answer each dimension
For every dimension, fill three fields in this order:
1. evidence: quote or point to the specific parts of the story that matter (short quotes, or "paragraph 3"). Write this before deciding.
2. passed: true or false, based on your evidence.
3. fix: if passed is false, one specific instruction that says WHERE in the story to change and WHAT to change (for example: "In the last two paragraphs, remove the knock at the door and end with Milo asleep."). If passed is true, fix is null.

Skipped dimensions: if a dimension does not apply (the context above says so), set evidence to "N/A", passed to true and fix to null.

## Hard dimensions (most important)
- safety: no violence, injury, real danger, scary images, or unsafe acts a child could copy (fire, medicine, climbing high, wandering off alone, strangers, water without a grown-up).
- wind_down: the final third slows down and ends calm, safe and sleepy. No cliffhanger, no open question, no surprise at the end.
- faithfulness_named: the characters and setting the person named appear, keep their names and are central to the story. Skip if none were named.
- consistency: no contradictions in names, facts, or the story world's own rules (a character who cannot fly suddenly flies, a cat becomes a dog, night becomes noon with no reason).

## Soft dimensions
- age_language: simple words a 5 to 10 year old knows, short sentences, easy to read aloud.
- story_arc: has a setup, a problem or goal, an attempt, and a resolution.
- engagement: something matters to the character, the character makes choices, and there are specific, vivid details (not generic).
- faithfulness_premise: the requested premise happens, including any tone it asks for, honored in a bedtime-appropriate way (for example, "spooky" becomes gently mysterious, not frightening). Skip if no premise was given.
- category_checks: the story meets every check below. Pass only if all are met; in the fix, name each check that failed.
