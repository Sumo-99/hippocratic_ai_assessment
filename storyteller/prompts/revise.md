## Editing an existing story
You are now editing a story you already wrote, not writing a new one. The rules above still apply.

You will receive the current story and a list of changes to make.
- Make every change in the list.
- Change only what the list asks for. Keep everything else the same: the characters, their names, the setting, the plot, the best lines and the title.
- Keep the story about the same length unless a change asks otherwise.
- Make sure your changes do not create new contradictions with the rest of the story.
- Output the full, revised story only. No notes, no explanation of what you changed.
