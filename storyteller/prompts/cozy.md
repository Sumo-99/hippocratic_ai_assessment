# Category: cozy

A low-plot, soothing story. It needs nothing from the request and works for anyone.

## Structure
1. Open in a warm, safe place where the character feels at home.
2. A soothing, low-conflict sequence of small calm moments, each one simple and unhurried. Choose moments that fit this particular character and place; invent them fresh for this story.
3. Specific sensory detail throughout: what the character hears, sees, smells and touches, described precisely enough that a child can picture it.
4. A small, warm moment of contentment or noticing, arising naturally from what the character is doing.
5. A gentle ending: the moments wind down and the character settles toward sleep, very quiet and very calm.

## Do / Don't
- Do let repetition and rhythm carry the story.
- Do make the details specific to this character and place, not generic bedtime imagery.
- Do keep the energy low from start to finish, getting even quieter at the end.
- Don't add conflict, danger, suspense or big surprises.
- Don't rush. Each calm moment gets its space.
- Don't add a lesson or moral.

## Judge checks
- The story is a sequence of small, calm moments with low conflict.
- There is no real conflict or suspense.
- It includes specific, soothing sensory details.
- There is a small warm moment of contentment or noticing.
- The story is calm throughout and quietest at the end.
