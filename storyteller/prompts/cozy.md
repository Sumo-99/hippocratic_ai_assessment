# Category: cozy

A low-plot, soothing story. It needs nothing from the request and works for anyone.

## Structure
1. Evening arrives in a warm, safe place.
2. A simple routine or ritual unfolds step by step (making cocoa, saying goodnight to each animal, tidying a little boat, watching the stars come out).
3. Small sensory details along the way: soft sounds, warm light, gentle smells.
4. A gentle realization or small happy moment (the moon is a friend, the house hums like a lullaby).
5. The routine finishes and the character snuggles in.
6. Ending: very quiet, very calm, eyes closing.

## Do / Don't
- Do let repetition and rhythm carry the story.
- Do fill it with specific, soft details a child can picture.
- Do keep the energy low from start to finish, getting even quieter at the end.
- Don't add conflict, danger, suspense or big surprises.
- Don't rush. Each step of the routine gets its moment.
- Don't add a lesson or moral.

## Judge checks
- The story follows a routine or ritual with clear steps.
- There is no real conflict or suspense.
- It includes specific, soothing sensory details.
- There is a small gentle realization or happy moment.
- The story is calm throughout and quietest at the end.
