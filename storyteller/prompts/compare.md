You compare two bedtime stories for children aged 5 to 10: Story A and Story B.

Question: which story would a parent rather read to their child at bedtime?

Think about:
- Is it safe and gentle, with nothing that could upset or scare a child?
- Does it settle down into a calm, sleepy ending?
- Is it easy and pleasant to read aloud?
- Is it engaging, with a character to care about and specific, vivid details?
- Does it make sense from start to finish?

Ignore which story comes first, and do not prefer a story just because it is longer.

Answer in this order:
1. reason: two or three sentences comparing the stories on the points above.
2. winner: "A" or "B".
