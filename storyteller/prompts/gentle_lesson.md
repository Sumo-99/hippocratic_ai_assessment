# Category: gentle_lesson

## Structure
1. Meet a likable character who holds a small mistaken belief or makes a small choice (not sharing, fibbing, rushing, leaving a friend out).
2. The choice plays out.
3. A gentle consequence follows: a small, kind, natural result (a game is less fun alone, a friend looks sad). Never a punishment.
4. The character notices, wonders and works it out themselves.
5. They make it right in a small, specific way.
6. Wind down: things are warm again, and the character settles into sleep feeling good.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep the character lovable the whole time. They are learning, not bad.
- Do let the character discover the realization through what they see and feel.
- Do keep the consequence small and repairable.
- Don't state the moral ("The lesson is...", "and so she learned that...").
- Don't let a grown-up lecture or scold.
- Don't shame the character or make the consequence harsh or scary.

## Judge checks
- There is a clear mistaken belief or small choice early on.
- The consequence is gentle and natural, not a punishment.
- The character reaches the realization themselves, not by being told.
- No stated moral, no lecture, no shaming.
- The ending is warm and calm.
